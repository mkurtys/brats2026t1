## Key Observations

1. **69.7% of lesions are pure ET** — no necrotic core. The TC=ET+NETC nesting assumed
   by region-based training applies to only 27% of lesions. Validates the decision against
   full region-based training for MET.

2. **38.6% of instances are sub-threshold (<27mm³)** — below the DSC evaluation cutoff
   but counted in detection F1. These tiny lesions are the main driver of false negatives.

3. **Pure RC (0.8%)** — isolated cavities with no ET/NETC context. Hardest RC cases for
   the model: no enhancing rim to anchor the prediction.

4. **ET+RC (2.0%)** — cavity adjacent to residual enhancing tumor. Model must learn to
   distinguish the non-enhancing void from surrounding ET.

5. **NETC almost always co-occurs with ET (27.3% NETC+ET vs 0.1% NETC-only)** — pure
   necrotic cores without enhancement are extremely rare. Loss weighting and sampling
   should treat NETC and ET as linked.
